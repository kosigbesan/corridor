The compliance trigger is live
California SB 253 phases Scope 3 in from 2027. Business travel and employee commuting are two of only five mandated categories, with limited assurance. About 2,596 US companies above $1B revenue are in scope.

Why these two categories

For most companies Cat 1 dwarfs everything. Travel and commuting are small by mass but high by control: the data sits in systems the company already owns, and behaviour moves the number inside a quarter.

That is the case for a live system of record rather than an annual upload.

The radiative-forcing landmine

Contrails and high-altitude NOx add about 69% to the CO2 number under DEFRA 2026, applied as a 1.7 multiplier to the CO2 component only.

Mixing with-RF and without-RF factors across years is the most common Cat 6 error. Corridor fixes the basis at the inventory level and refuses to blend.

Why this is the moat

Anyone can show a number. The defensible product is the trail: factor versioning, restatement logic, and a rebase test that fires when a methodology change moves the total by more than 5%.

It is unglamorous, and it is what an assurer asks for first.
